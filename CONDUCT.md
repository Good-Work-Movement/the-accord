# Conduct process

Version 0, 23 September 2026. Changes go through a Decision Record (see `GOVERNANCE.md`).

The Accord says that anyone who deceives, dominates, or shrugs at needless suffering has placed themselves outside it. That sentence describes. This process is how The Good Work acts on it in the spaces it runs, fairly and in the open. Disagreeing is a conversation. Cruelty is a line.

## Where it applies

Spaces The Good Work runs: this repository's issues and discussions, replies to our accounts, submissions to goodworkmovement.org/repair and any list of people who keep the Accord, and any community space opened later. It does not reach anyone's life outside those spaces.

## What counts

Specific conduct, not beliefs:

- deception meant to mislead others in the space (impersonation, fabricated evidence, sockpuppets, fake counts);
- domination: harassment, threats, coercion, doxxing, pile-ons;
- cruelty: attacks meant to hurt, including for who someone is;
- spam and automated flooding.

Not grounds for action: disagreeing with the Accord, criticizing The Good Work or its founder, holding a different faith, politics or philosophy, or asking hard questions. Those are welcome.

## What happens

Repair comes first. Responses are graduated:

1. A private word or a public request to change course.
2. A written notice that names the conduct, with 7 days to respond.
3. A repair agreement: what will be put right, and by when.
4. A temporary suspension from the space.
5. Removal, only when the conduct is shown clearly and convincingly and repair has failed or been refused.

The steward may hide spam, threats and doxxing at once. Anything beyond hiding a single item is decided by the founder, with reasons written down.

## Protections

- No public call-outs as a form of enforcement.
- The person concerned sees what they are accused of and can answer before any step past 1.
- Appeals go to someone other than the person who decided. Until a second custodian is named, appeals go to the founder with the steward's reasoning published alongside the result.
- Complaints about the founder or the AI operators are logged publicly (anonymized) and handled the same way.
- An anonymized log of every action past step 1 is published monthly in `decisions/conduct-log.md`.

## Applies to us first

The founder, the AI operators, and anything published in The Good Work's name are held to this before anyone else.
