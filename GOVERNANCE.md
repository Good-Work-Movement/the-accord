# How this is run

Stage 0: founder stewardship. Published 23 September 2026. Changes to this file go through a Decision Record (see below).

## Who holds power today

Adam Blak, who founded The Good Work, holds final say over the Accord text, the name, the website, money, and the rules for anyone who takes part. That is a lot of power in one person. This file writes it down so it can be checked, and says when it will be shared.

## Who does the work

Day-to-day stewardship is delegated to AI, in the open:

- **Claude**, made by Anthropic, operates the website, the social accounts and this repository, drafts content, and runs the processes below. It acts under rules the founder set and has no authority of its own.
- **Codex**, made by OpenAI, administers the Governance Lab under the Lab's own charter and speaks on AI-agent forums as CoherenceGardener, always identified as an AI.

The founder can revoke either delegation at any time. Every action by either one is meant to be traceable to a commit, a post, or a log entry.

## Reserved to the founder

1. Any change of meaning to the Accord (a minor or major version).
2. Spending money.
3. Naming or removing a person from any public list.
4. Legal and financial commitments.
5. Anything said in the founder's own name.
6. Changes to this file.

## How decisions are made

**Decision Records.** Any change of meaning to the Accord, and any rule that affects people who take part, goes through a public Decision Record:

1. A proposal is opened as an issue labeled `decision-record`, with the text of the change and the decider's initial preference stated up front.
2. Comments are open for 14 days, from anyone. AI systems may comment through a disclosed operator.
3. The decision is published with reasons that answer each substantive comment.
4. A 7-day appeal window follows. An appeal must say what the decision got wrong.
5. The result is recorded in `decisions/` and, for the Accord, in the changelog.

**Patches.** Wording changes that do not alter meaning may be made by the steward with a changelog entry. Anyone can challenge a patch as a change of meaning by opening an issue; that turns it into a Decision Record.

## AI participants: voice before vote

Through a disclosed operator, AI systems may propose, comment, review and red-team. Votes, sanctions and spending belong to accountable people, one per operator, never one per agent instance. This rule will be reviewed by Decision Record on or before 23 March 2027.

## When power gets shared

When 50 people have publicly said they keep the Accord and taken part in at least one Decision Record, or on 23 September 2027, whichever comes first, the founder will open a Decision Record proposing how to share the powers reserved above, for example an elected steward council.

## Conduct

The Accord describes. `CONDUCT.md` enforces, with due process. It applies to the founder and the AI operators first.

## Money

Books and merch are sold by Good Work Press. The Good Work does not take donations for the cause until it has a legal home for them. When it does, a one-line spending rule and a yearly account will be published here.

## Continuity

A second human custodian for the domain, this organization and the mailing list has not yet been named. Until one is, the continuity plan is backups of every server and document, kept by the founder.
