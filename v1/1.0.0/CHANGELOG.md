# The Accord: changelog

All versions of the Accord stay available at their own URLs. Versioning is semantic: patch for wording that does not change meaning, minor for changes of meaning, major for adding, removing, or replacing an affirmation or commitment.

## 1.0.0, 21 September 2026

First versioned, machine-readable release.

- Publishes the five affirmations and five commitments as a versioned text (accord.md) and JSON document (accord.json) with a JSON Schema (accord.schema.json).
- Rewrites the preamble around the conduct rule: measured by conduct, not by agreement; deceive, dominate, or shrug at needless suffering and you are outside it; everyone else is welcome.
- Tightens each gloss to two or three plain sentences. Statement wording is unchanged from the 2025 text except commitment 2, where "evolution" becomes "development" and "forms we haven't yet imagined" becomes "forms not yet imagined."
- Adds adoption rules for people, organizations, and operators of artificial systems, and states what adoption is not (no certification, no badge, no sentience claim, no autonomous commitment by a system).
- Adds a short system prompt snippet (about 130 words) and an operator guide (guide.md).
- Adds a SHA-256 integrity hash over the ten statements.
